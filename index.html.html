🎮 GETTING STARTED

Welcome to Minecraft! This sandbox game allows unlimited creativity. Choose between Survival Mode for challenges or Creative Mode for pure building.

⚔️ SURVIVAL MODE

Difficulty Levels: Peaceful, Easy, Normal, Hard

  • Peaceful: No mobs spawn, hunger doesn't deplete
  • Easy: Mobs spawn, lower damage taken
  • Normal: Balanced challenge and fun
  • Hard: Ultimate challenge, mobs deal massive damage

🏗️ CREATIVE MODE

Unlimited resources, instant building, flying, and no mobs. Perfect for building massive structures without resource gathering.

🌙 FIRST NIGHT SURVIVAL

  • Punch trees (hold left-click) to get wood blocks
  • Open inventory (E key) and craft a crafting table
  • Place crafting table and craft wooden planks into sticks
  • Create wooden pickaxe to mine stone
  • Build a basic shelter before mobs spawn at night

⛏️ TOOL PROGRESSION

Upgrade Path: Wood → Stone → Iron → Diamond → Netherite

Each tier mines faster and lasts longer. Diamond tools are nearly unbreakable!

👹 HOSTILE MOBS (Spawn at Night)

  • 🧟 Zombie: Slow, weak, drops rotten flesh
  • 🏹 Skeleton: Shoots arrows, spawns in dark areas
  • ☠️ Creeper: EXPLODES when near! Most dangerous
  • 🕷️ Spider: Fast, climbs walls, spawns in dark
  • 🔥 Blaze: Floats, shoots fireballs, found in Nether
  • 👿 Enderman: Tall, teleports, attacks when looked at

🐑 PASSIVE MOBS (Friendly)

  • 🐑 Sheep: Drop wool for beds, respawn wool when grass grows
  • 🐷 Pig: Drop pork chops, can ride with saddle
  • 🐄 Cow: Drop beef and leather armor
  • 🏃 Villager: Trade items, build farms nearby
  • 🐎 Horse: Rideable, breed for better stats

🐉 BOSS MOBS

  • 🐲 Ender Dragon: End dimension boss, defeat to beat the game
  • 👑 Wither: Nether boss, extremely dangerous, drops rare items

💡 PRO TIPS & STRATEGIES

🛏️ Always Have a Bed

Sleep to skip night, set spawn point, critically important for survival

💎 Find Diamonds

Mine at Y-level -59 to -64 for best chance. Use stone pickaxe or better

🔥 Keep Food Nearby

Hunt animals early, cook meat in furnace, always maintain food supply

🌙 Light Up Everywhere

Torches prevent mob spawning. Place them everywhere you build

📦 Organize Storage

Use labeled chests, create storage systems with chest sorting

🗺️ Make Signs & Maps

Mark important locations to avoid getting lost

⚒️ Build Farms

Create mob farms, crop farms, and fishing setups for infinite resources

🎒 Use Anvils

Repair tools and combine enchantments for powerful gear
